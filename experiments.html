A collection of experiments in multi-sensory interaction, prototyped with AI. Made for fun, but also to explore and test what’s possible, as each of them could become a micro product.

Sound Kit

A playable keyboard of 26 voices. Type, tap the pads or wave at the camera, and every letter becomes a sound and a shape on screen.

Sound · Gesture · Camera · Claude Code · 2026

Try it here ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Beat Sequencer

A step sequencer where each beat sends a ripple through a small solar system. Load your own sounds, add bars and change the tempo as it plays.

Sound · Rhythm · Motion · Figma Make · 2025

Kinect Keys

Press any key to play. Every note blooms into a circle of colour, so a melody slowly fills the screen with bubbles.

Sound · Visuals · Google AI Studio · 2025

Emoji Paint Studio

A retro paint program where the brushes are emoji and your hand is the mouse. One finger draws, a fist stops, two fingers swap the brush. There is a kaleidoscope mode, of course.

Gesture · Camera · Riff · 2025

Air Graffiti

Spray paint in thin air. The camera follows your finger, the paint drips down the wall, and the can hisses as you draw.

Gesture · Camera · Sound · Riff · 2025
